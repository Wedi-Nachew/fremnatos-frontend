# Master Data Spreadsheet Guide

Create one Excel or Google Sheet with these tabs.

**Tab 1: General Info**
Legal Name, Founded Year, Motto Tigrinya, Motto English, Brand Colors, Logo Files, Social Media Links.

**Tab 2: Metrics**
Metric Name, Demo Value, Real Value, Data Source, Date Verified, Verified By, Used In Page.

**Tab 3: People**
Name, Role, Phone, Email, Hours, Photo File, Consent ID, Used In Page.

**Tab 4: Branches**
Branch Name, City, Address, Phone, Hours, Programs, Capacity, Demand, Waitlist, Photos, Map URL.

**Tab 5: Bank Accounts**
Bank, Account Number, Account Name, Active, Verified By, Date.

**Tab 6: Partners**
Partner Name, Category, Website, Logo File, Permission, Contact.

**Tab 7: Testimonials**
Name, Role, Program, Quote, Photo, Video, Consent ID, Page.

**Tab 8: News**
ID, Title, Date, Category, Author, Excerpt, Image, Full Text, Published.

**Tab 9: Media**
Filename, Page, Section, Category, Date, Consent ID.
