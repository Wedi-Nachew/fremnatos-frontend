# Fremnatos — Gallery Photo & Video Submission Guide
**Purpose:** Collect real, consent-cleared media to replace all placeholder (`picsum.photos`) images.  
**Who fills this out:** Communications Lead + Program Managers + Photographers.  
**Priority:** HIGH

---

## Submission Rules

1. **Consent is mandatory.** Every identifiable person in a photo or video must sign the Consent Form (`10-consent-form-template.md`).
2. **File naming:** `Category_Page_Section_YYYYMMDD.jpg`
   - Example: `Children_Homepage_AboutUs_20250915.jpg`
3. **Minimum resolution:** 1920×1080 for hero images, 800×600 minimum for all others.
4. **Formats:** JPG or PNG for photos. MP4 (H.264) for videos.
5. **No watermarks** unless they are the organization's own official watermark.
